Claw machine example assets
===========================

Cabinet, articulated claw, bear and elephant:
  Third-party models prepared for this example, with the cabinet's front
  glass hidden and pastel toy materials. They keep their original license
  terms, which the repository's source-code license does not change; they
  came without a separate license document.

Bunny and T2 toy:
  Prepared from earlier Newton demonstration assets, whose terms apply.

simulation.npz:
  The tetrahedral rest meshes of the 25 toys, their per-tetrahedron
  materials, and two states, positions and velocities of particles and
  bodies: settled at 6 s and with the claw open over the bear at 10.5 s.

Visuals:
  visuals.usdc: visual surfaces and display colors, source Z-up metres,
  shifted by the origin in scene.json; rigid mesh points are body-local.
  visuals.json: body and toy of each surface, material factors and textures.
  skin_bindings.npz: how each toy's visual surface follows its tetrahedra,
  for rendering only.
  PNG files: textures of the materials.
