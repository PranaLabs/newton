Urban claw-machine example assets
=================================

Cabinet, articulated claw, bear and elephant:
  Derived from the user-supplied ClawMachineUrban_Newton_Handoff_v1.zip,
  including the prepared front-glass-hidden cabinet and pastel toy materials
  used in the accepted green-bear demonstration.
  These supplied third-party model assets retain their original license terms;
  the repository source-code license does not relicense the models or textures.
  The handoff did not include a separate license document.

Bunny and T2 toy:
  Prepared toy_bunny_demo and toy_t2_demo meshes used in the same demonstration,
  derived from the previously available Newton demo assets. Their upstream
  asset terms continue to apply.

Prepared simulation:
  Exact rest mesh and per-tetrahedron material field from the accepted
  four25_green_bear9_deep_20261025 scene. The checkpoints are the original
  settled state at 6.0 s and the 2 mm contact run's open-gripper state at 10.5 s. Positions
  AND velocities are included; the original rest geometry remains unchanged.

Visuals:
  visuals.usdc: original visual surfaces and display colors, source Z-up metres,
  shifted by scene.json origin. Rigid mesh points are body-local.
  visuals.json: body/toy mappings, material factors, optional texture paths.
  skin_bindings.npz: original shared-surface gathering or convex FEM anchors
  with polar-rotated residuals. Bindings affect rendering only.
  PNG files: textures extracted from the supplied/prepared GLB materials.

No generated animation, video, full trajectory cache or asset-generation script
is required to run the example. The two compact checkpoints are optional starts.
