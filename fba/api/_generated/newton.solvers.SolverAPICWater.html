newton.solvers.SolverAPICWater#

class newton.solvers.SolverAPICWater(model, config=None)[source]#

Bases: SolverBase

Incompressible water with a free surface, by APIC particles on a MAC grid with a cut-cell pressure solve.

The water is the active particles of the model (ParticleFlags.ACTIVE), each carrying particle_mass / density of volume. Each step transfers them to the faces of a fixed MAC grid by the affine particle-in-cell method (APIC, quadratic B-splines), adds gravity, solves the pressure of the fluid cells so that the velocity is divergence-free on the parts of the faces outside the solids, and transfers the velocity back to the particles, which then move, leave the solids, and keep apart:

  • The solids are the shapes of a rigid model taken by setup_colliders(): their signed distances at the grid’s nodes give each face’s open fraction (the variational cut-cell method of Batty, Bertails and Bridson, 2007), the static shapes’ once, the bodies’ at each step from their poses. A closed part of a face moves with its body.

  • The free surface is the particles’ level set (Zhu and Bridson, 2005); the pressure vanishes on it through the ghost fluid, and cells with particles are always fluid.

  • The pressure solve takes a fixed number of conjugate gradient iterations with a Jacobi preconditioner, one reduction per iteration (Chronopoulos and Gear), its sums in a fixed order. A global correction keeps the liquid’s volume at the particles’ volume, and particles closer than their rest spacing are pushed apart.

  • The pressure’s impulse on each body over the step, body_impulse, couples the water to a rigid solver explicitly: add_body_forces() applies it in the body solver’s next step.

  • Particles that leave the grid’s box, or turn non-finite, leave the water: they become inactive. Streams (add_stream()) bring inactive particles back in.

A step reads nothing back to the host and allocates nothing, so that it records into a CUDA graph. It needs a CUDA device. The time step should keep the particles within a cell or two per step. Sums of particle quantities onto the grid use atomic additions, so that steps differ from run to run by rounding.

Call register_custom_attributes() on the ModelBuilder of the particles before building the model: it adds the per-particle APIC matrix apic_water:particle_qd_grad to the states.

The bodies’ poses and velocities come from state_in.body_q and state_in.body_qd, which the caller sets to the rigid model’s state arrays, as for SolverImplicitMPM’s colliders.

Parameters:

  • model (Model) – The model of the water particles.

  • config (SolverAPICWater.Config | None) – The solver’s configuration, see SolverAPICWater.Config.

Raises:

ValueError – On a CPU device, a model without the custom attributes, or an empty grid box.

Layers of faces into the air that take the water’s velocity for the transfer back.

max_fluid_cells: int | None = None#

Fluid cells that the pressure solve holds; None takes the particle count, at most the grid’s cells. Fluid cells past it stay air; SolverAPICWater.dropped_fluid_cells() reports them.

Conjugate gradient iterations of the pressure solve per step.

Part of the overlap of two particles closer than 0.8 times their spacing that a step removes; 0 turns the separation off.

Part of the liquid’s relative volume error, against the particles’ volume, that a step corrects.

voxel_size: float = 0.01#

Cell size [m] of the grid; two particles per cell edge give the water its best surface.

add_body_forces(body_f, dt)#

Add the water’s last impulse on each body, body_impulse, to body_f as a constant wrench over a step of dt [s]: before the rigid solver’s next step, for the explicit coupling.

add_stream(origin, direction, flow, side_count=3, start=0.0, stop=None)#

Add a stream of water, as from a spout: from start until stop [s] of the solver’s time, which its steps advance, layers of side_count x side_count particles one spacing apart leave origin along direction at the speed that carries flow [m^3/s], one layer per spacing of travel. A step’s layers take inactive particles in index order and join at the step’s end, where their flight from origin puts them; the stream waits while too few particles are inactive.

Parameters:

  • origin – The stream’s origin [m].

  • direction – The stream’s direction; its length does not matter.

  • flow (float) – The stream’s flow [m^3/s].

  • side_count (int) – Particles across each side of the stream’s square section.

  • start (float) – When the stream starts [s].

  • stop (float | None) – When the stream stops [s], or None for never.

dropped_fluid_cells()#

The most fluid cells that a step since the last call left out of the pressure solve, past Config.max_fluid_cells; it reads the device.

notify_model_changed(flags)#

The particles’ flags and masses are read at each step; the colliders change through setup_colliders().

setup_colliders(model, shapes=None)#

Take the shapes shapes of the rigid model model, by default those that collide with particles (ShapeFlags.COLLIDE_PARTICLES), as the water’s solids.

The static shapes’ signed distances at the grid’s nodes are found now. Each body’s shapes give a distance volume in the body’s frame, found now on a grid of half a cell, from which each step reads the nodes’ distances at the body’s pose in state_in.body_q. A later call replaces the colliders.

Parameters:

  • model (Model) – The rigid model whose shapes the water meets, on the water’s device.

  • shapes (list[int] | ndarray | None) – The indices of its shapes to take, or None for those that collide with particles.

Raises:

ValueError – For a shape type without a signed distance here (heightfields, Gaussians, planes on bodies).

step(state_in, state_out, control, contacts, dt, *, observables=None)#

Advance the water by dt [s] from state_in to state_out, which may be the same state.

Parameters:

  • state_in (newton.State) – The particles at the step’s start, with body_q and body_qd of the colliders’ bodies when shapes on bodies collide.

  • state_out (newton.State) – The particles at the step’s end.

  • control (Control | None) – Unused.

  • contacts (Contacts | None) – Unused.

  • dt (float) – The time step [s].

  • observables (Observables | None) – Unused; the solver reports no observables.

property body_impulse: array#

The water’s pressure impulse on each body of the colliders’ rigid model over the last step, shape (body_count,), dtype spatial_vector: the linear impulse [N s] and the angular impulse about the body’s center of mass [N m s], in the world frame.
