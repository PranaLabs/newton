Simulate the model's tendons (`ModelBuilder.add_tendon`) in `SolverFBA`: it routes them with Newton's shared tendon routing and holds each group of spans between two anchors with one unilateral row on its length, solved with the rigid contacts, so a string pulls but never pushes and takes no force while slack. The guides' capstan friction is linearized onto the first anchor's slide, the spans' compliance and damping make the row a spring and damper on a taut string's stretch, and the custom attributes `fba:guide_friction_force` (a friction force that does not grow with the load), `fba:tendon_time` and `fba:tendon_damping_ratio` (softness relative to the row's effective mass, as MuJoCo's `solref`) add FBA's settings. The guides between a group's anchors must be on static or kinematic bodies.
