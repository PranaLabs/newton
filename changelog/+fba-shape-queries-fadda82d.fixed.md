Fix `SolverFBA`'s own shape queries to match Newton's particle contacts: face and rod-segment contacts took barrel cylinders as straight (3.8 cm off at the bulge of a 15 cm barrel), segment contacts read the shapes' `COLLIDE_PARTICLES` flags once, so a shape switched on later never took them, and particles of no world skipped the shapes of worlds. Particle contacts now hold particles to their shapes' surface velocity, `Contacts.soft_contact_body_vel`, of conveyors and animated meshes, and the contact buffers grow by half again, so that a climbing contact count does not reallocate them and recapture their graphs at every step.
