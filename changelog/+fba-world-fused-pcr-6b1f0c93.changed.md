Speed up `SolverFBA`'s lite contact solve with several worlds on CUDA: each world's sparse PCR iterations now run fused over its chunks of rows, with its own scalars, row budget and stop flag, instead of one reduction block per world. The fused path takes up to `max(1024, max_n)` chunks of 256 rows over all worlds, so models of more than 1024 small worlds also run fused. Results change by rounding only; no migration is needed.
