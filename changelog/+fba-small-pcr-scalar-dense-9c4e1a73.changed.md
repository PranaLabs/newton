Run the capped PCR iterations of more small `SolverFBA` contact systems in one CUDA launch, as those of small systems in 3x3 blocks already run: sparse systems of at most 512 rows that hold at most 64 entries per row on average, such as the contacts of a few rods with `nsn_sparse_storage=True`, and dense systems of at most 128 active rows in the steps recorded into a CUDA graph, chosen on the device. `elastic_cords_fba` takes 18.9 ms per frame in place of 23.9 ms and `rod_gripper_fba` 14.1 ms in place of 17.3 ms on an RTX 5090. The launch adds its sums in another order, so results differ by rounding.
