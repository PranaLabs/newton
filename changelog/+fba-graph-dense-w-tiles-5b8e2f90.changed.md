Speed up the products by the dense `W` of `SolverFBA` steps recorded into CUDA graphs with the full operator: they take the active contacts' rows and the 128-square tiles of `W` that hold nonzero entries alone, the matrix product adds each row once, and the PCR iteration takes three launches in place of five, its sums as the five launches add them. Replayed from a graph per frame, `desk_cables_fba` takes 5.6 ms per frame in place of 7.4 ms, `particle_rods_fba` 7.4 ms in place of 8.6 ms and `hemp_rope_fba` 3.8 ms in place of 4.2 ms on an RTX 5090. The matrix product adds its sums in another order, so results differ by rounding.
