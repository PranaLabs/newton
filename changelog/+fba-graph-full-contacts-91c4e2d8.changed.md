Record `SolverFBA` steps with particle-shape contacts and the full operator (dense `W`, at most 25 fixed `nsn_linear_iterations`) into CUDA graphs: the step counts its active contacts on the device and takes, by a conditional graph node, the smallest of the sizes 64, 256, 1024 and up to 2048 contacts that holds them, building that size's dense `W` on the device; `dropped_contacts` reports active contacts past 2048.
