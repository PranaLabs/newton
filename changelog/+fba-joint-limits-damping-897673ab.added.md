Add joint limits and passive joint damping to `SolverFBA`: the free axis of a revolute or prismatic joint takes its limits (`Model.joint_limit_lower`, `Model.joint_limit_upper`) as hard unilateral rows, solved with the rigid contacts when the axis comes near a limit, and its `Model.joint_damping` as an implicit damper row. A revolute axis needs both limits, less than 2 pi apart. Joints at the default infinite limits and zero damping run as before.
