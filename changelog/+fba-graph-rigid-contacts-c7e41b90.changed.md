Record `SolverFBA` steps with rigid contacts into CUDA graphs, in a model without particles, in one world, with `nsn_sparse_storage` and at most 25 fixed `nsn_linear_iterations`: every slot of the rigid contact buffer becomes a contact's three rows, the joint rows that contacts touch are selected on the device, and the pattern of `W` takes a storage of 32 blocks per slot, so that the recorded steps match the steps outside a graph bit for bit; `dropped_contacts` reports the contacts a step cuts when its pattern outgrows the storage.
